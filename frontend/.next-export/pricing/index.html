common questions

Are there placement commissions?

No. The protocol charges a flat monthly fee. There are zero placement commissions or success fees taken from either employer or clinician.

Can clinicians really use this for free?

Yes, forever. Clinicians connect, verify, and find shifts at zero cost. The platform is funded by employer and recruiter subscriptions.

What happens after my trial ends?

You will be prompted to choose a plan. If you do not upgrade, your node is paused — all your data is retained for 30 days.

Is there a contract lock-in?

No. All plans are monthly and cancellable at any time. Enterprise plans have optional annual pricing with a 20% discount.
