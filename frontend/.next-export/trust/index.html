trust protocol.

mednode.cloud operates on a zero-compromise security architecture. every interaction — credential, payment, identity — is protected by sovereign cryptographic protocols.

security pillars

CRYPTO_v4.2

end-to-end encryption

All data transmitted between nodes is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with perfect forward secrecy. No plaintext ever leaves your device.

VERIFY_v3.1

credential verification

Every clinician credential — GMC registration, DBS, indemnity — is verified against live regulatory databases before being added to the mesh.

PRIVACY_v2.8

zero-knowledge privacy

Employers never see personal data until a match is confirmed. Candidate identity is cryptographically masked during the discovery phase.

AUDIT_v1.9

AI audit trail

Every AI-assisted decision is logged with full explainability. Hiring decisions are auditable, transparent, and compliant with NHS and CQC standards.

COMPLY_v5.0

GDPR & CQC compliance

The protocol is designed to be compliant with UK GDPR, Care Quality Commission guidelines, and NHS Digital Data Security standards from the ground up.

SOVEREIGN_v2.4

sovereign data ownership

Your data belongs to you. Delete your node at any time and all associated data is cryptographically erased from the mesh within 72 hours.
